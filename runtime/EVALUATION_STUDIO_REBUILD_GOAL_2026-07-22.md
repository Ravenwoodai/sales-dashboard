# Evaluation Studio Rebuild Goal

Work continuously for up to eight elapsed hours, or until the scoped work is genuinely complete, to rebuild Sales Dashboard Evaluation Studio as a controlled validation laboratory.

## Startup

- Read `AGENTS.md` and every required startup document in order.
- Read `docs/LOCAL_MODEL_CAPABILITY_POLICY.md`, the current `runtime/LOCAL_MODEL_CAPABILITY_REGISTER_*.json`, `runtime/SESSION_HANDOVER.md`, and the evaluator failure/remediation audits needed to understand the history.
- Inspect the worktree and preserve unrelated user changes.
- Follow repository model-escalation rules before high-risk writes.

## Non-negotiable boundary

- Preserve fail-closed behavior. Do not submit, resume, retry, poll, or harvest any Qwen/local-model job.
- Create no new Qwen Spiel version, decomposition, successor, or semantic rescue lane.
- Promote no capability and restore no model-backed routes, scores, queues, rankings, coaching, lead/CRM actions, or semantic fields.
- Keep historical jobs, runs, prompts, templates, knowledge, and results immutable, research-only, authority `none`, and operationally excluded.
- Unsupported operational records remain `unknown`/`not_scored`; create no routine human-review queue.
- Human labels are permitted only as benchmark truth.

## Deliverables

### 1. Capability Register and archive

Make the Studio landing page show every capability's exact scope, provenance, status, authority, exclusions, evidence, failure reason, and permitted next action. Put historical content in an unmistakable read-only Historical Research area. Expose no execution or mutation controls. Never imply that technical success, JSON validity, copied quotes, confidence, or historical output equals semantic authority.

### 2. Benchmark Builder

Add research-only frozen manifests with stable fingerprints, complete prior-audit exclusion, genuinely unseen calls, balanced positives, hard negatives and unsupported cases, plus separate smoke, development, promotion and shadow partitions. Any call that influenced rules, prompts, labels, or reconciliation cannot count as independent evidence.

### 3. Human Labelling

Add a benchmark-only direct-quote interface with sufficient chronological context, precise confirmation/correction capture, and no more than five examples per batch. Freeze labels and evidence before candidate inference. Keep this separate from operational queues.

### 4. Candidate comparison and promotion

Compare deterministic rules and any future materially different candidate field by field. Show false positives, false negatives, abstentions, unsupported cases, evidence integrity, critical errors, category results, resource use, and stop-rule status. Require predeclared evidence, exclusions, thresholds, critical limits, budgets, and external approval. Ten calls are smoke only; support approximately 50 development and at least 100 genuinely unseen promotion examples, with larger sets for rare critical cases. Run no model during this goal. A failed narrow unseen semantic gate stops that candidate family instead of causing further decomposition.

### 5. First trusted lane: deterministic voicemail and inbound linkage

Without a model, measure exact voicemail, exact approved callback-request wording after a trustworthy prompt, chronology validity, stable lead and salesperson linkage, later inbound observed, elapsed time, and original-salesperson answer/overflow only where source facts prove it. Show sale and gross profit only when CRM supplies them. Never infer callback causation, receptiveness, sale, or conversion. Corrupt chronology, missing stable IDs and ambiguity become `unknown`/`not_scored`. Recompute verified July facts from source; do not hard-code totals.

### 6. UI trust

Clearly separate Source Fact, Deterministic Derived Fact and Semantic Judgment. Explain unavailable states, provide exact definitions and evidence drilldowns, follow `docs/UI_DESIGN_STANDARD.md`, verify responsive/error/empty/loading states, and remove any active-looking unpromoted-AI wording or control.

### 7. Verification and governance

Add focused tests for capability locks, archive immutability, manifest integrity, frozen labels, quote verification, voicemail/inbound facts, unknown handling, mutation rejection and misleading UI. Re-run the adversarial mutation set, `npm run audit:trusted-boundary`, and the full test suite. Start the app safely and browser-check Evaluation Studio and relevant drilldowns. Update specifications, architecture, plans, quality, decisions, run log, handover, backlog and capability register only when behavior genuinely changes. Never mark promotion without a separate completed gate and explicit approval.

## Execution behaviour

Use small reversible changes, inspect each phase and correct regressions. Work autonomously within scope and do not pause for non-blocking preferences. Do not commit, push, publish, contact outsiders, spend model tokens, or redesign unrelated areas. If work requires new authority, production inference or weaker safeguards, document that blocker and continue other safe work. Finish with completed changes, verification evidence, remaining risks and the exact next step.
